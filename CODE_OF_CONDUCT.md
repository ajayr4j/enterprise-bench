# Code of Conduct

Enterprise-Bench follows the Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and benchmark quality.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported using the contact channel listed in `SUPPORT.md`. Maintainers will review reports and respond in a way that is fair, timely, and respectful of privacy.
